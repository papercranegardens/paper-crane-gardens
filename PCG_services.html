What We Offer

Our Services

Naturalistic landscape design, installation, and ecological maintenance for Portland homeowners who want an outdoor space that feels soft, layered, and genuinely alive.

Services & Pricing
01

Landscape Design

For homeowners ready to transform their outdoor space from the ground up. Our signature service covers everything: site analysis, concept development, planting design, hardscape planning, and material selection. We design with naturalistic plant communities, permeable hardscape materials — wood, clay, natural stone — and a bias toward softness over structure. Projects are built to feel discovered, not delivered.

From $1,200 Pricing depends on size and scope Inquire
02

Landscape Installation

Full-service installation — demolition and yard waste removal, plant and material procurement, planting, soil amending, and mulching. We source naturally derived materials: reclaimed wood, clay, decomposed granite, and uncut stone selected to age beautifully and settle into the landscape rather than dominate it. Includes post-install care instructions and access to discounted ongoing maintenance rates.

From $1,500 Pricing depends on size and scope Inquire
03

Ecological Landscape Maintenance

Monthly fine gardening for Portland homeowners who want a beautifully maintained naturalistic landscape without the hassle. Every visit is seasonally informed and ecologically grounded — hands-on horticultural care that supports the soft, layered character of your garden. Not a mow-and-blow service. Not even close.

  • Seasonally appropriate pruning, deadheading, and soil conditioning
  • Organic soil management & composting strategies
  • Mulching, hand weeding & ecological weed suppression
  • Integrated pest & disease management (organic solutions only)
  • Seasonal planting & rotation of annuals/perennials
  • Irrigation programming & efficiency optimization
  • Native plant & pollinator habitat care
Custom Pricing Annual contract required. Inquire
04

Garden Consultation

For homeowners in the early stages, or those who want a professional eye and a clear direction. A focused, single-session consultation for Pacific Northwest homeowners to assess your space, discuss your vision, and leave with a clear sense of direction. Ideal if you're not ready for a full project but want to stop second-guessing yourself. On-site or virtual — and never unprepared. Before we arrive, we research your site thoroughly and prepare a curated plant palette and design direction specific to your conditions and vision. Your first conversation starts at a level of depth most clients don't reach until the third meeting. Ideal for DIYers, budget-conscious homeowners, and anyone considering a larger project. Perfect for those who want the Paper Crane aesthetic — naturalistic, soft, coastal-inspired — but aren't sure where to start. Consultation fee credited toward full design services.

$150 / hour Inquire

Please note: We do not mow lawns, apply pesticides, herbicides, or chemical controls of any kind. Our Integrated Pest Management practices rely exclusively on organic and mechanical solutions. We serve residential clients throughout the Portland Metropolitan Area.
