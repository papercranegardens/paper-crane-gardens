About Paper Crane
"A garden's highest achievement is looking like it never needed us."

Paper Crane Gardens brings together two coastlines: the warmth and ease of coastal California, and the lush, layered plant palette of the Pacific Northwest. The result is something Portland doesn't see enough of — outdoor spaces that feel genuinely calm. Wispy grasses catching afternoon light. Natural wood and clay underfoot. Native plant communities that move with the wind and deepen with every season. Ecological by practice, beautiful by design, and grounded in certified horticultural expertise.
